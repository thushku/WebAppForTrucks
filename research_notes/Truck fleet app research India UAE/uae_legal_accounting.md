## 1. VAT (Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 as amended), including 2025-2026 changes

### Takeaway
VAT is 5%, with mandatory registration at AED 375,000 and voluntary at AED 187,500. Domestic road haulage is standard-rated. International transport of goods (start or end outside the UAE) is zero-rated. A 2025 FTA clarification says a domestic leg is zero-rated only when the same supplier provides the international carriage. Amendments effective 1 Jan 2026 (Federal Decree-Law No. 16 of 2025) remove self-invoicing for reverse charge, add a 5-year limit on carrying forward or refunding excess input tax, and let the FTA deny input VAT linked to evasion. Salik tolls and Dubai parking have carried 5% VAT since 1 June 2026, which changes how trip expenses must be booked.

### Cited Findings
**Registration**
- Mandatory VAT registration threshold: AED 375,000. Voluntary threshold: AED 187,500. Both are tested on taxable supplies and imports over the previous 12 months, or expected in the next 30 days — [FTA – Registration for VAT](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/vat/vat.topics/registration.for.vat.aspx); [FTA](https://tax.gov.ae/en/content/registration.for.vat.aspx)
- A non-resident making taxable supplies in the UAE must register from its first taxable supply, with no threshold (third-party firm, not FTA wording) — [Avyanco 2026 guide](https://avyanco.com/vat-registration-requirements-in-the-uae-a-complete-2026-guide/)

**Transport zero-rating**
- The GCC VAT Framework Agreement lets member states zero-rate international transport of goods and passengers. Article 33 of the UAE VAT Executive Regulation (Cabinet Decision No. 52 of 2017) zero-rates international goods and passenger transport, meaning carriage that starts or ends outside the UAE, including domestic legs that form part of an international journey — [Bloomberg Tax](https://www.bna.com/insight-vat-gccare-n57982094528/?amp=true)
- Article 45 of Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 lists zero-rated supplies, including international transport and the means of transport with related goods and services — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/pay-zero-vat-on-these-goods-services-in-the-uae); [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/hszr5r84-president-issues-vat-decree-law). (These sources point to the statute's Art. 45 list, while Bloomberg points to the Exec. Reg. Art. 33 conditions. Both are probably right: Art. 45 holds the list and Art. 33 the detailed conditions.)
- **FTA Public Clarification VATP040 (2025):** the domestic leg of an international goods movement is zero-rated **only if the same supplier provides the international transport**. A subcontractor carrying only the UAE leg for another carrier charges 5% — [PwC tax alert on VATP040](https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/tax/documents/2025/tax-alert-public-clarification-VATP040.pdf)

**Amendments effective 1 January 2026**
- Federal Decree-Law No. 16 of 2025 (VAT) and No. 17 of 2025 (Tax Procedures) were published on 14 Nov 2025 — [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/tax/united-arab-emirates-new-federal-decree-laws-amending-the-vat-law-and-tax-procedures-law); [Baker McKenzie alert](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/12/united-arab-emirates-vat-and-tax-procedures-law-amendments). Most other outlets name only No. 16 for the VAT changes — [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/gulf-tax-insights/2025/gulf-tax-insights-december-2025/uae-announces-amendments-to-vat-law-effective-1-january-2026)
- **Reverse charge:** amended Art. 48(1) means taxpayers no longer issue tax invoices to themselves for reverse-charge imports of goods or services. The duty to account for reverse charge stays, and supporting documents must be kept as the Executive Regulation specifies. A self-invoice may still help support input-tax recovery when supplier documents are missing — [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/gulf-tax-insights/2025/gulf-tax-insights-december-2025/uae-announces-amendments-to-vat-law-effective-1-january-2026); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uae-vat-law-amendments-january-1-2026); [Crowe](https://www.crowe.com/ae/news/uae-vat-law-and-tax-procedures-law)
- **5-year limit:** excess recoverable input tax can be carried forward or refunded only within 5 years from the end of the tax period in which it arose. Under a transitional rule, where that window has already lapsed or lapses within one year of 1 Jan 2026, refund requests may be filed until **31 Dec 2026** — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/economy/2025/12/03/uae-amends-vat-rules-to-simplify-tax-procedures/); [China/Middle East Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/china-outbound-news/uae-issues-new-vat-amendments-effective-jan-1-2026)
- **Anti-evasion:** the FTA must deny input VAT where the supply, or a chain of supplies, was connected to tax evasion and the recipient knew it. It may also deny recovery where the recipient should have known — [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/gulf-tax-insights/2025/gulf-tax-insights-december-2025/uae-announces-amendments-to-vat-law-effective-1-january-2026); [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/12/united-arab-emirates-vat-and-tax-procedures-law-amendments)

**Tax invoices, simplified invoices and credit notes** (secondary sources summarising Exec. Reg. Arts. 59-60)
- Art. 59(1) lists the particulars of a full tax invoice (one summary counts 12). Art. 59(2) allows a simplified tax invoice with fewer particulars (one summary counts 5) — [TaxBox – Art. 59 text portal](https://tax.lynchpinconsulting.com/taxlaws/tax-invoices-article-59-vat-executive-regulations/); [Farahat & Co](https://farahatco.com/blog/information-included-tax-invoices-uae/)
- A simplified tax invoice may be issued when the recipient is not VAT-registered, or when the recipient is registered and consideration does not exceed **AED 10,000**. Sources disagree on whether the AED 10,000 cap also applies to unregistered recipients. Reverse-charge supplies cannot use a simplified invoice — [Farahat & Co](https://farahatco.com/blog/information-included-tax-invoices-uae/); [Paci](https://paci.ae/blogs/uae-vat-invoice-format-mandatory-fields/); [UAE Expert Hub](https://www.uaeexperthub.com/uae-tax-invoice-requirements/)
- Art. 60 sets the particulars of a Tax Credit Note — [RVG UAE](https://rvguae.com/vat/the-framework-for-tax-invoices-and-credit-notes-in-the-uae-vat-system/)
- A tax invoice must be issued within 14 days of the date of supply. The penalty for failing to issue one is AED 2,500 per detected case — [UAE Expert Hub](https://www.uaeexperthub.com/uae-tax-invoice-requirements/) (secondary)
- Once a business is on e-invoicing (mandatory or voluntary), secondary sources say simplified-invoice and paper credit-note formats will no longer be allowed for in-scope transactions — [Crowe – amendment to VAT Exec Reg](https://www.crowe.com/ae/news/what-is-the-recent-amendment-to-uae-vat); [Invoice Data Extraction blog](https://invoicedataextraction.com/blog/uae-vat-invoice-requirements) (vendor; verify)

**Tolls and parking now carry VAT (trip-expense impact)**
- Salik charges 5% VAT on toll tariffs and tag-activation fees from **1 June 2026** under Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017. Peak goes from AED 6 to AED 6.30 and off-peak from AED 4 to AED 4.20 — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/salik-announces-5-vat-from-june-1-on-toll-tariff-tag-activation); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/05/22/dubai-road-toll-fees-to-rise-from-june-1-as-salik-imposes-tax-charges/)
- The FTA required Salik to apply VAT retrospectively from 1 July 2022. RTA is compensating Salik; The National reports AED 471m including penalties. Sources disagree on whether the retrospective period ends 31 Mar or 31 May 2026 — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/05/22/dubai-road-toll-fees-to-rise-from-june-1-as-salik-imposes-tax-charges/); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/salik-announces-5-vat-from-june-1-on-toll-tariff-tag-activation)
- Parkin (Dubai parking) charges 5% VAT on all parking services from 1 June 2026 — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/parkin-announces-5-vat-to-all-parking-services-starting-june-1); [Crowe](https://www.crowe.com/ae/news/uae-vat-applies-to-salik-and-parking-fees)

### Inferences (what the software must do)
- **VAT rate engine per trip/invoice line:** at minimum Standard 5%, Zero-rated (international transport), Exempt, Out-of-scope, and Reverse-charge. A trip/job needs a "transport type" flag (domestic / international-export / international-import / domestic leg of an international move). It should also record whether the company is the **principal carrier** for the whole international move or a **subcontractor**, because a subcontractor's UAE leg is 5% under VATP040. Keep evidence fields for zero-rating: origin/destination country, customs/exit documents, bill of lading or CMR number, border crossing.
- **Registration-threshold monitor:** rolling 12-month and forward 30-day taxable turnover against AED 375,000 and AED 187,500, with alerts.
- **Tax invoice generator:** carry all Art. 59 particulars (see Gaps for the field list to confirm), apply the AED 10,000 simplified-invoice rule, block simplified invoices for reverse-charge supplies, track the 14-day issue deadline, and issue credit notes per Art. 60 linked to the original invoice. All of this must also map to the e-invoicing data model (section 3).
- **Reverse charge:** book output and input VAT on imported services or goods (e.g., foreign software, foreign repairs, GCC tolls) **without** generating a self-invoice from 2026. Store supporting documents.
- **Input-tax ageing:** track the 5-year window on excess input tax, flag the 31 Dec 2026 transitional deadline, and add a supplier-risk field (input tax may be denied for evasion-linked chains).
- **Expense VAT recovery:** Salik/toll and Parkin parking costs from 1 June 2026 should be booked as VAT-bearing (recoverable input VAT if the fleet is registered and holds a valid tax invoice/statement from Salik), and before that date as out-of-scope. Expense capture needs a date-aware VAT rule per expense category, plus a flag for Salik/RTA retrospective adjustments.

## 2. Corporate Tax (Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022)

### Takeaway
Small Business Relief (revenue ≤ AED 3m) was due to end with tax periods ending 31 Dec 2026. **Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026 (announced 7 Aug 2026) extends it to tax periods ending on or before 31 Dec 2029.** Accounting standards scale with revenue: cash basis up to AED 3m, IFRS for SMEs up to AED 50m, full IFRS above that. Audited financial statements are required above AED 50m revenue and for all Qualifying Free Zone Persons. CT records must be kept for 7 years. Transfer-pricing master and local files apply only at AED 200m entity revenue or AED 3.15bn group revenue. A TP disclosure form applies once related-party transactions exceed about AED 40m (secondary source).

### Cited Findings
- **SBR extension:** MoF Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026 extends SBR to tax periods ending on or before **31 Dec 2029**, replacing the earlier 31 Dec 2026 end — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/uae-extends-corporate-tax-relief-for-small-businesses-until-2029); [The National, 7 Aug 2026](https://thenationalnews.com/business/economy/2026/08/07/uae-extends-corporate-tax-relief-for-small-businesses-until-2029); [Aletihad/WAM](https://en.aletihad.ae/news/uae/4683761/ministry-of-finance-extends-small-business-relief-for-corpor)
- SBR threshold stays at **AED 3m revenue** (Ministerial Decision No. 73 of 2023). It is lost if revenue exceeded AED 3m in any earlier tax period since 1 June 2023. QFZPs and members of MNE groups with consolidated revenue > AED 3.15bn are excluded. SBR must be **elected in the CT return** and is not automatic. Registration, filing and record-keeping duties continue — [Entrepreneur ME](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/business-news/uae-extends-small-business-tax-relief-through-2029); [Osome](https://osome.com/ae/blog/small-business-relief-extended-2029/)
- The 2029 cut-off is tested on the business's **tax period end date**, not the calendar year — [Osome](https://osome.com/ae/blog/small-business-relief-extended-2029/)
- **Accounting standards (Ministerial Decision No. 114 of 2023):** full IFRS above AED 50m revenue; IFRS for SMEs at or below AED 50m; cash basis allowed where revenue ≤ AED 3m without applying to the FTA. Above AED 3m, accrual basis applies unless the FTA approves exceptional circumstances — [Henry Club](https://www.henryclub.ae/banking-finance/ifrs-reporting-uae/); [QAS Pro Global](https://qasproglobal.com/uae-bookkeeping-laws-3-federal-regulations-2026/); [Bestax](https://bestaxca.com/guide-financial-reporting-uae/)
- **Audited financial statements:** mandatory where revenue > AED 50m in the tax period, or for any QFZP regardless of revenue. Sources tie this to Ministerial Decision No. 82 of 2023, and some cite a "Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025" — [QAS Pro Global](https://qasproglobal.com/uae-corporate-tax-return-documents-2026/); [Bestax](https://bestaxca.com/maintain-accounting-records-uae-corporate-tax/). (The decision number is unclear; see Gaps.)
- **Record retention:** **7 years** after the end of the relevant tax period — [Bestax](https://bestaxca.com/maintain-accounting-records-uae-corporate-tax/)
- **Transfer pricing documentation (Ministerial Decision No. 97 of 2023):** master file and local file required where the taxable person's revenue ≥ **AED 200m**, or it is in an MNE group with consolidated revenue ≥ **AED 3.15bn** — [MoF press release](https://mof.gov.ae/ministry-of-finance-issues-decision-on-transfer-pricing-documentation-requirements-for-corporate-tax-purposes); [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/research/requirements-maintaining-transfer-pricing-documentation.html); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/uae-corporate-tax-in-transfer-pricing-update-companies-with-at-least-dh200m-must-main-documents-1.1683788736447)
- **TP disclosure form** (filed with the CT return): primary threshold of aggregate related-party transactions > **AED 40m**, then categories > **AED 4m** must be disclosed — [Bestax](https://bestaxca.com/transfer-pricing-documentation-uae/) (secondary; verify)

### Inferences (what the software must do)
- **Accrual double-entry general ledger** with an IFRS / IFRS-for-SMEs chart of accounts. A cash-basis mode can be offered only to entities with revenue ≤ AED 3m. Most fleets of 20+ trucks will exceed AED 3m, so accrual is the default.
- **Revenue tracker per tax period** that flags: AED 3m (SBR eligibility and cash basis, tested against all prior periods since 1 June 2023), AED 50m (full IFRS, audit, e-invoicing tier), AED 200m (TP files).
- **Related-party tagging** on customers, suppliers, owners and directors (owner-drivers, family-owned leasing companies, sister fleets), with a running total of related-party transaction values against AED 40m and AED 4m per category. Fleets often lease trucks from owners' other companies, so this matters.
- **Financial statements pack** (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, notes data), a fixed-asset register with depreciation for trucks and trailers, and exports for auditors and the EmaraTax CT return.
- **7-year immutable retention** of all books and source documents (trip sheets, fuel receipts, toll statements). Adopting 7 years as the single retention floor covers VAT's (unverified) 5 years too.
- **Free-zone entity mode:** QFZP flag, qualifying vs non-qualifying income split, and the audit requirement (see Gaps).

## 3. E-invoicing (Ministerial Decisions No. 243 and 244 of 2025, as amended in 2026)

### Takeaway
UAE e-invoicing is a Peppol-based "5-corner" model. Businesses cannot submit directly: every B2B/B2G e-invoice goes through an MoF-accredited service provider (ASP) in PINT-AE (UBL 2.1 XML), and the ASP reports data to the FTA. The pilot and voluntary phase started **1 July 2026**. Businesses with revenue **≥ AED 50m** must appoint an ASP by **30 Oct 2026**, extended in May 2026 from 31 July 2026, and go live **1 Jan 2027**. Businesses **< AED 50m** must appoint by **31 Mar 2027** and go live **1 July 2027**. Government entities go live 1 Oct 2027. A SaaS for fleets will need to integrate with one or more ASPs, or become one.

### Cited Findings
- MoF issued Ministerial Decision No. 243 of 2025 (framework) and No. 244 of 2025 (phased implementation) on 29 Sept 2025. Both take effect on publication in the Official Gazette — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/10/uae-framework-scope-implementation-e-invoicing-system.html); [EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2025-1964-uae-ministry-of-finance-publishes-ministerial-decisions-on-e-invoicing); [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/10/united-arab-emirates-new-e-invoicing-rules)
- The VAT Executive Regulation was amended alongside these decisions to implement e-invoicing — [Baker McKenzie InsightPlus](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/tax/united-arab-emirates-new-ministerial-decisions-and-amended-vat-executive-regulation-for-implementing-the-e-invoicing-system)
- **Pilot:** selected taxpayers, by written agreement with MoF/FTA, from **1 July 2026**. Voluntary adoption is open to others who meet the technical requirements — [Andersen](https://ae.andersen.com/insights/E-Invoicing%20Alert.pdf); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/10/uae-framework-scope-implementation-e-invoicing-system.html)
- **Original timeline (MD 244/2025):** revenue ≥ AED 50m: appoint ASP by 31 July 2026, implement by 1 Jan 2027. Revenue < AED 50m: appoint ASP by 31 Mar 2027, implement by 1 July 2027. Government entities: appoint ASP by 31 Mar 2027, implement by 1 Oct 2027 — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/10/uae-framework-scope-implementation-e-invoicing-system.html); [EY](https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2025-1964); [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/10/united-arab-emirates-new-e-invoicing-rules)
- **2026 change:** on 10 May 2026 MoF announced amendments moving the ASP appointment deadline for revenue ≥ AED 50m from 31 July 2026 to **30 Oct 2026**. **Go-live stays 1 Jan 2027** — [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/uae-e-invoicing-asp-appointment-deadline-extended-but-go-live-remains-01012027.html); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/tax-news/uae-e-invoicing-deadline-extended-what-this-means-for-businesses-now-1.500535992); [RTC Suite](https://rtcsuite.com/uae-e-invoicing-update-new-asp-appointment-deadline-for-large-businesses/). (Most sources say the amendment revised MD 244/2025. One cites "Ministerial Decision No. 66 of 2026", unverified. Gulf News puts the old deadline at 1 July 2026 instead of 31 July.)
- **Model and format:** Peppol 5-corner network. Each invoice must pass through an MoF-accredited ASP and cannot go directly to the FTA. Format is PINT-AE (UAE Peppol International invoice specification on UBL 2.1 XML). The ASP sends tax data to the FTA (corner 5) — [Wafeq](https://www.wafeq.com/en-ae/tax-and-reporting/uae-fta-e-invoicing-what-your-accounting-system-must-support-before-the-deadline); [Infinite IT](https://infinite-it.com/en/blog/peppol-e-invoicing-uae-guide); [Billingo](https://www.billingo.com/en-ae/resources/read/peppol-e-invoicing-uae); [ClearTax](https://www.cleartax.com/ae/e-invoicing-uae) (vendor sources, consistent with each other)
- **Penalties (Cabinet Decision No. 106 of 2025)** apply only from a person's mandatory date: failure to implement or appoint an ASP AED 5,000 per month or part month; late issue or transmission of an e-invoice AED 100 per invoice, capped at AED 5,000 per calendar month; failure to update the ASP on data changes AED 1,000 per day — [e-invoice.app](https://www.e-invoice.app/guides/uae-e-invoicing); [Flick Network](https://www.flick.network/en-ae/e-invoicing-uae) (vendor sources; one other source gives an inconsistent AED 2,500-50,000 range, so verify against the Cabinet Decision text)
- One vendor blog says MoF issued "Electronic Invoicing Guidelines V1.0" on 23 Feb 2026 — [Invoice Data Extraction](https://invoicedataextraction.com/blog/uae-e-invoicing-mandate-2026) (unverified)

### Inferences (what the software must do)
- **Tiering logic:** classify each tenant by annual revenue (≥ / < AED 50m) and show its ASP-appointment and go-live dates. Fleets of 100+ trucks may well cross AED 50m and must be live **1 Jan 2027**, which is 2.5 months after this research date. The product therefore needs an e-invoicing path ready, or a documented ASP-partner integration, for large tenants in Q4 2026.
- **Build vs partner:** a SaaS cannot transmit directly to the FTA. Options are (a) integrate with one or more MoF-accredited ASPs (API hand-off of PINT-AE XML, receiving status, clearance IDs and inbound invoices), or (b) apply to become an ASP, which is heavy. Option (a) is the practical default.
- **Data model:** invoice and credit-note schema mapped 1:1 to PINT-AE (UBL 2.1). It needs buyer and seller TRN / Peppol participant IDs, line-level VAT category codes (S, Z, E, O, reverse charge), item classification, currency and exchange rate, invoice-type codes (tax invoice, credit note, self-billing, etc.), and references from credit notes to original invoices. Inbound e-invoices from suppliers (fuel companies, workshops, Salik) must also be **received** and auto-posted to AP.
- **Lifecycle controls:** issue deadlines, retry and queue on ASP failure (late transmission costs AED 100 per invoice), an audit log, and alerts when master data changes (company name, TRN, address), since failing to update the ASP costs AED 1,000 per day.
- **Simplified invoices** may disappear for in-scope B2B flows. Keep simplified/B2C templates configurable but off by default for e-invoicing tenants.

### Gaps

## 4. Payroll and labour (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021; WPS; ILOE; Emiratisation)

### Takeaway
Mainland private-sector employers (all MOHRE-licensed transport companies) must follow FDL 33/2021 on hours, overtime, leave, gratuity and deductions, and must pay salaries through **WPS**. Under **Ministerial Resolution No. 340 of 2026 (in force 1 June 2026)**, the previous month's wages are due **on the 1st of the following month**, and compliance requires paying at least **85%** of wages on time. Deductions, including advance and loan recoveries, are capped at a 50% total, with sub-caps for specific deduction types. Expatriate drivers must hold ILOE unemployment insurance (AED 5 or AED 10 per month). Transport and storage firms with 20-49 staff fall under Emiratisation quotas.

### Cited Findings
**Scope**
- FDL 33/2021 took effect 2 Feb 2022, replacing Federal Law No. 8 of 1980. It covers mainland private-sector employees and excludes government and domestic workers. DIFC and ADGM have their own employment laws — [Simmons & Simmons](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/ckwkiifv11g300b24k8yb6jg1/top-15-changes-to-the-federal-uae-labour-and-employment-laws); [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2021/12/united-arab-emirates-updates-federal-labour-law); [ZenHR](https://www.zenhr.com/en/uae-labor-law)

**Hours and overtime**
- Standard working time is 8 hours a day and 48 hours a week. After more than 5 consecutive hours, breaks totalling at least 1 hour are required (Art. 18) and do not count as working time — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/legal/uae-breaks-during-work-hours-mandatory-under-employment-law?amp=1); [Zoho Payroll](https://www.zoho.com/en-ae/payroll/academy/payroll-compliance/uae-labour-laws.html)
- Ramadan hours are reduced by 2 hours a day — [greytHR](https://www.greythr.com/middle-east/blog/working-hours-in-uae-and-ksa-2026/)
- Overtime is paid at **125%** of basic wage, and **150%** for overtime between 10 pm and 4 am. It is calculated on basic salary. Overtime is capped at **2 hours a day** unless work is needed to prevent serious loss — [Hyring](https://hyring.com/free-hr-toolkit/hr-glossary/uae-labour-law/); [ZenHR](https://www.zenhr.com/en/uae-labor-law); [Farahat & Co](https://farahatco.com/blog/payroll-uae-what-international-businesses-know)
- **Midday outdoor work ban:** no outdoor work in direct sun from 12:30 pm to 3:00 pm, **15 June-15 Sept**, with shade and cooling required. A secondary source reports fines of AED 5,000 per worker, up to AED 50,000. Ministerial Resolution No. 122 of 2026 adds accommodation standards for employers with 50+ workers earning ≤ AED 1,500 a month — [greytHR 2026](https://www.greythr.com/middle-east/blog/uae-labour-law-2026/); [HiveDesk](https://hivedesk.com/compliance/united-arab-emirates)

**Leave**
- Annual leave is **30 calendar days** a year (Art. 29). With 6-12 months' service it accrues at 2 days a month — [ZenHR](https://www.zenhr.com/en/uae-labor-law); [Hyring](https://hyring.com/free-hr-toolkit/hr-glossary/uae-labour-law/)
- **Sick leave (Art. 31):** up to **90 days a year** after probation: the first 15 days at full pay, the next 30 at half pay, the remaining 45 unpaid. It is unpaid during probation or if caused by misconduct. The employee must notify within 2 days — [u.ae (official)](https://beta.government.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/types-of-leaves-and-entitlements-in-the-private-sector/sick-leave); [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/industries/healthcare/uae-sick-leave-how-much-can-you-take-and-can-you-be-fired-if-you-are-ill)
- Bereavement and parental leave are 5 days each — [Simmons & Simmons](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/ckwkiifv11g300b24k8yb6jg1/top-15-changes-to-the-federal-uae-labour-and-employment-laws)

**End-of-service gratuity (Art. 51)**
- After ≥ 1 year of continuous service: **21 days' basic wage per year for the first 5 years**, then **30 days' basic wage per year** after that. Total is capped at **2 years' wages** — [Simmons & Simmons](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/ckwkiifv11g300b24k8yb6jg1/top-15-changes-to-the-federal-uae-labour-and-employment-laws); [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2021/12/united-arab-emirates-updates-federal-labour-law); [Hyring](https://hyring.com/free-hr-toolkit/hr-glossary/uae-labour-law/)

**Deductions from wages (Art. 25)**
- Deductions are allowed only in listed cases. Loan or advance recovery needs the worker's **written consent**, **no interest**, and must stay within the monthly cap. **Total deductions must not exceed 50% of wages.** Damage caused by worker error or violation of instructions: no more than **5 days' wages per month** unless a court approves more. Recovery of overpayments: ≤ **20%** of wage. Court-ordered debts: ≤ **25%**, except alimony — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/when-can-private-companies-deduct-salaries-in-the-uae-1.500012178); [Gulf News Q&A](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/new-uae-labour-law-when-can-my-employer-deduct-my-wages-1.1637847186119); [Zoho Payroll](https://www.zoho.com/en-ae/payroll/academy/compliance/salalry-deductions-minimum-wage-and-pay-rules-in-uae.html); [Addleshaw Goddard](https://www.addleshawgoddard.com/link/19da673553184e508d03a21ebb4aa539.aspx)
- One ministry-based summary caps disciplinary **fines** at 5% of wages, separate from the 5-day damage cap — [Zoho Payroll](https://www.zoho.com/en-ae/payroll/academy/compliance/salalry-deductions-minimum-wage-and-pay-rules-in-uae.html) (verify against Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022)

**Wage Protection System (WPS)**
- **Ministerial Resolution No. 340 of 2026** (MOHRE), in force **1 June 2026**, replaces the 2022 framework (MR 43/2022). It applies to MOHRE-licensed private-sector employers; free-zone WPS rules may differ. Each month's wages are due on the **1st day of the following month** (previously the 15th, with a grace period). Payment after that is "delayed". The compliance threshold rose from 80% to **85% of total wages due**. Escalation includes work-permit suspension, administrative penalties and possible criminal referral. The grace period for new employees was removed — [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/06/uae-introduces-stricter-wage-protection); [Morgan Lewis LawFlash](https://morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/05/uae-introduces-new-wage-protection-system-resolution-effective-1-june-2026); [Morgan Lewis blog](https://morganlewis.com/blogs/shiftingsandsoflaborlaw/2026/05/uae-tightens-wage-protection-rules-with-new-wps-resolution-effective-june-2026); [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/06/uae-new-resolution-impacting-payroll-immediate-action-required); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/uae-sets-monthly-salary-deadline-for-private-sector-from-june-1-1.500544328)
- One secondary guide gives the escalation days as notifications from Day 2, new work-permit suspension from Day 5, and fines or downgrade from Day 11. Repeat violators with 50+ workers may face prosecution referral, asset seizure and travel bans — [FIT](https://www.fit.ae/news-and-blogs/wps-uae-complete-guide); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/did-your-payday-change-in-uae-what-private-sector-workers-must-know-1.500545698) (thresholds inconsistent across sources)
- **SIF (Salary Information File):** made up of one **SCR** (Salary Control Record, the header with employer establishment ID, totals and period) and one **EDR** (Employee Detail Record) per employee. EDR fields include employee MOL/person ID, agent/bank routing code, IBAN/account, pay period start and end, days in period, fixed income, variable income and leave days. EDR totals must reconcile **exactly** with the SCR; even AED 0.01 off causes rejection. Currency is AED. The file name is the 13-digit employer establishment ID plus a creation timestamp. Banks and exchange-house agents add their own variations — [Zoho – SIF format](https://www.zoho.com/en-ae/payroll/academy/compliance/wps-sif-file-format.html); [Mashreq WPS file format guide](https://netbanking.mashreqbank.com/B001/WPS_File_Format_eng.html); [Mynd](https://www.myndsolution.com/best-practices/processing-wps-compliant-salaries-payroll-processing-uae/)

**ILOE (unemployment insurance)**
- Premium: basic salary ≤ AED 16,000 pays **AED 5 a month** (benefit cap AED 10,000 a month); > AED 16,000 pays **AED 10 a month** (cap AED 20,000). 5% VAT reportedly applies on top. Fines: **AED 400** for not subscribing, **AED 200** for unpaid premiums of more than 3 months (policy can be cancelled). Benefit is 60% of average basic salary for up to 3 months after 12 months' enrolment. I found no 2026 change — [LY Lawyers](https://lylawyers.com/blog/the-unemployment-insurance-uae-overview); [Movingo](https://movingo.ae/blog/iloe-insurance-uae-guide); [Henry Club](https://www.henryclub.ae/business-operations/iloe-insurance-uae/)
- ILOE is an employee obligation, but employers may pay it.

**Emiratisation (affects transport companies)**
- Ministerial Resolution No. 455 of 2023 extends Emiratisation to firms with **20-49 employees** in 14 activities, including **Transportation and Storage**: 1 UAE national by 31 Dec 2024, 2 by 31 Dec 2025. Penalties reported as AED 96,000 per unfilled position for 2024, rising to AED 108,000 (Jan 2026) for 2025 targets. Sources do not agree fully. I found no 2026 target for this group — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/uae-expands-emiratisation-to-private-sector-firms-with-less-than-50-employees-1.96897597); [Ruya Careers](https://ruyacareers.ae/post.aspx?id=22500); [Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/en/article/emiratisation-2026-the-complete-employer-guide-quotas); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/private-sector-warned-to-meet-emiratisation-goals-or-face-penalties-1.500359140)
- The "AED 6,000" figure is a monthly non-compliance contribution per unhired citizen (rising by AED 1,000 a year to 2026), **not** a minimum wage — [Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/en/article/emiratisation-2026-the-complete-employer-guide-quotas)

### Inferences (what the software must do)
- **Pay structure:** separate **basic** salary from allowances (housing, transport, food, trip/mileage allowance). Overtime, gratuity and leave pay are all computed on **basic**, so misclassifying allowances inflates or deflates statutory amounts.
- **Overtime engine:** 125% normal and 150% for 22:00-04:00, using trip and GPS timestamps or timesheets. Warn when overtime exceeds 2 hours a day, and flag rest-break gaps (no 5 consecutive hours without a 1-hour break). Configure Ramadan hours (minus 2 hours a day) and the **midday-break calendar** (12:30-15:00, 15 Jun-15 Sep) for helpers doing outdoor loading. Drivers in air-conditioned cabs may be treated differently; see Gaps.
- **Leave module:** 30-day annual accrual (2 days a month in months 6-12), a sick-leave ladder (15 full / 30 half / 45 unpaid, unpaid during probation), and bereavement and parental leave. Unused leave goes into final settlement.
- **Gratuity calculator and provision:** 21/30-day formula on last basic wage, 2-year cap, a monthly accrual journal (IAS 19 / IFRS for SMEs s.28 provision), and a final settlement statement.
- **Advances and deductions engine:** record the driver's **written consent** to each advance or loan (signed upload or e-sign), charge **no interest**, and schedule instalments. Enforce caps per payslip: total ≤ 50% of wage; damage ≤ 5 days' wage a month; overpayment recovery ≤ 20%; court debts ≤ 25%. Traffic fines a driver incurred in a company truck should only be recovered within these limits and with a documented basis (see Gaps). Block payroll runs that breach the caps.
- **WPS SIF export** with exact SCR/EDR reconciliation, bank/agent routing codes and per-bank templates. **Pay-date controls for MR 340/2026:** default the payroll calendar so funds reach WPS by the **1st of the following month**, show the 85% on-time KPI per month, and warn from Day 2 onward.
- **ILOE tracker** per employee (policy number, tier, expiry) and Emiratisation headcount monitor (company size 20-49 or 50+, UAE nationals employed against target).
- Pay **bonus** and **variable pay** through WPS EDR variable fields, not cash, so they count toward the 85% test.

### Gaps
- **Notice period** (unverified background: 30-90 days per Art. 43) and the **144 hours per 3 weeks** overtime ceiling (unverified) were not confirmed this session.
- Sector-specific hours exceptions for transport workers or drivers in the Executive Regulation (Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022) were not found.
- **Minimum wage:** I found no general UAE minimum wage for private-sector expatriate workers. The only "AED 6,000" figure found relates to Emiratisation non-compliance contributions. Unverified background: MOHRE sets a minimum salary for Emiratis counted toward quotas.
- **GPSSA pension** for UAE-national employees (unverified background: Federal Decree-Law No. 57 of 2023; private-sector contribution about 26% of contribution salary, split roughly employer 12.5% / employee 11% / government 2.5%, but the split varies by emirate and date) was not verified. If a fleet employs Emiratis, payroll must compute and remit GPSSA contributions.
- 2025-2026 amendments to FDL 33/2021 itself (e.g., Federal Decree-Law No. 9 of 2024 on dispute jurisdiction, any 2025 changes) were not researched because the search budget ran out.
- Whether employers can pass traffic fines on to drivers through payroll deductions, and on what legal basis under Art. 25, needs counsel review.

## 5. Driving hours, rest and truck movement bans (Federal Traffic Law, RTA Dubai, ITC Abu Dhabi)

### Takeaway
I found **no UAE statutory hours-of-service, fatigue or tachograph regime** for heavy-vehicle drivers. Experts have campaigned for EU-style caps, and some logistics firms apply 9 hours a day / 4.5 hours continuous as internal policy. Driver hours are therefore limited mainly through the Labour Law (8 hours a day, overtime cap, breaks). Time-of-day **truck movement bans** apply and are tightening. Dubai introduced new restrictions from **1 Oct 2026**: Sheikh Zayed Road 06:00-22:00, urban roads in three peak windows, some roads 24/7, and Emirates Road exempt. Abu Dhabi bans heavy vehicles at peak times (fine AED 1,000 + 4 black points) and has rerouted trucks off E11/E10 since 1 Dec 2025. The new Federal Traffic Law (FDL 14/2024) has been in force since 29 Mar 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Federal Decree-Law No. 14 of 2024 on Traffic took effect **29 March 2025**, repealing Federal Law No. 21 of 1995. Penalties go up to AED 200,000 and imprisonment for serious offences — [uaelegislation.gov.ae (text)](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2598/download); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/transport/new-uae-traffic-law-starts-tomorrow-eight-key-fines-and-rules-drivers-must-know-1.500075348); [u.ae road safety](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/road-safety)
- No binding UAE hours-of-service rule was found. Road-safety experts have campaigned for a cap. One logistics firm's internal policy is 9 hours total, 4.5 hours continuous, and 15-minute breaks after 2 hours. The EU benchmark is 9 hours a day / 56 hours a week — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/safety-a-heavy-responsibility?amp=1)
- **Dubai, from 1 Oct 2026 (RTA + Dubai Police):** trucks barred **06:00-22:00** on Sheikh Zayed Road, Beirut Street and residential areas (Al Mizhar, Muhaisnah, Oud Al Muteena). On urban roads such as Airport St, Oman St and Damascus St the bans are **06:30-08:30, 13:00-15:00 and 17:30-20:00**. **24/7 bans** apply on Al Qudra St, Al Meydan St, all crossings and Airport Tunnel. **Emirates Road is exempt.** Essential movements need an RTA "Mobility Permit for Heavy Vehicles in Banned Times and Roads" (Right-of-Way department) — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c2fwakt-rta-dubai-police-announce-truck-movement); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/transport/dubai-to-introduce-truck-movement-restrictions-from-october-1-with-emirates-road-exempt-1.500688610); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/trucks-movement-restriction-dubai-roads-from-october-1); [Gulf Today](https://www.gulftoday.ae/news/2026/09/26/rta-dubai-police-announce-truck-movement-restrictions-on-roads-across-dubai-from-oct1)
- **Abu Dhabi:** heavy vehicles are banned from roads in peak hours **06:30-09:00 and 15:00-18:00** (Al Ain has separate windows). Violation costs **AED 1,000 + 4 black points** — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/abu-dhabi-heavy-vehicles-banned-during-peak-hours-violators-to-be-fined?amp=1); [Dubai Eye](https://dubaieye1038.com/news/local/aed-1000-fine-for-violating-abu-dhabis-truck-movement-hours) (article dates not shown; may predate 2025)
- Abu Dhabi has banned trucks from Sheikh Maktoum bin Rashid Road (E11) and Al Raha Beach Road (E10) since **1 Dec 2025**, with peak-hour truck restrictions on Al Rawdah Road (E30) in Musaffah — reported in [Khaleej Times – UAE traffic law changes](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/uae-traffic-laws-changes-residents-must-know?amp=1) (attribution approximate; verify with ITC)

### Inferences (what the software must do)
- **Driver-hours tracking** from GPS and trip data: driving time, continuous driving, breaks and daily total. Default alert thresholds should follow the Labour Law (8 h + max 2 h overtime; 1 h break after 5 h), with optional best-practice limits (9 h driving, 4.5 h continuous). Label these as company policy, not law.
- **Truck-ban rules engine / geofences:** load Dubai (from 1 Oct 2026) and Abu Dhabi ban windows as editable, emirate-specific, time-based geofences. Warn at trip planning when an ETA falls in a ban window, and alert live from GPS. Store RTA mobility-permit details (permit no., validity, roads) per vehicle so permitted trucks are exempted.
- **Fine attribution:** link ban-violation fines (with black points) to driver, vehicle and trip by timestamp (see section 6).
- Rules change often (Dubai Oct 2026, Abu Dhabi Dec 2025), so these must be **data-driven configuration**, not code.

### Gaps
- No primary RTA or ITC document was read, so exact road lists, Ramadan ban windows, and Sharjah/Ajman/RAK truck bans were not researched.
- Whether FDL 14/2024 or its Executive Regulation contains heavy-vehicle driver rest rules, tachograph or speed-limiter mandates could not be confirmed. A vendor blog claims mandatory GPS-linked speed limiters with AED 10,000 fines, but this is unverified and internally inconsistent — [NextBillion](https://nextbillion.ai/blog/gcc-heavy-vehicle-rules-compared-a-fleet-compliance-guide).

## 7. Heavy-vehicle compliance (registration, inspection, insurance, permits, tracking, weights)

### Takeaway
Federal weight and dimension limits come from Cabinet Resolution No. 138 of 2023 (implementing Federal Decree No. 12 of 2023). Gross limits run from 21 t (2 axles) to 65 t (6+ axles), with fines of AED 400 per excess tonne, AED 1,500 per overloaded axle and AED 3,000 for over-dimension. Its implementation was postponed in early 2024, and the current enforcement status is unclear. Dubai RTA has required telematics devices on heavy trucks since 2017, phased by vehicle age and weight. There is also a federal truck and shipment tracking system under ICP. Mulkiya, inspection and insurance rules were not researched (search budget exhausted).

### Cited Findings
- **Weights and dimensions (Cabinet Resolution No. 138 of 2023):** maximum gross weight 2 axles 21 t, 3 axles 34 t, 4 axles 45 t, 5 axles 56 t, 6+ axles **65 t**. Single steering axle 8 t; single non-steering axle 13 t. Length: rigid 12.5 m, tractor + semi-trailer 21 m, truck + trailer + semi-trailer 28 m. Width 2.6 m. Height 4.6 m (4.75 m for car carriers) — [MoEI announcement (31 Jan 2024)](https://www.moei.gov.ae/en/media-center/news/31/1/2024/table-of-maximum-weights-and-dimensions-of-heavy-vehicles-on-federal-roads-announced); [uaelegislation.gov.ae](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2312/download)
- **Fines:** AED 400 per tonne (or part) over the gross limit (base tier, < 10% excess); AED 1,500 per axle or tandem group over the axle limit; AED 3,000 for exceeding dimensions. Repeat weight violations within a month can lead to vehicle confiscation for up to 30 days — [uaelegislation.gov.ae](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2312/download). A secondary source adds tiers of AED 500 (10-20% over) and AED 600 (> 20%), capped at AED 15,000 — [Dubizzle](https://www.dubizzle.com/blog/cars/heavy-vehicle-traffic-fines-uae/) (not in the official excerpts seen)
- **Status conflict:** the Cabinet postponed implementation (planned Q1 2024) and ordered a study — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/transport/uae-postpones-heavy-vehicle-weight-limit-rule-1.101130112). The legislation portal shows effective date 1 Feb 2024, status "active" — [uaelegislation.gov.ae](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2312/download). u.ae says vehicles > 65 t are banned "from 2024" — [u.ae road safety](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/road-safety). Current enforcement is unresolved.
- **Dubai RTA telematics:** a mandatory phase began in 2017 for heavy trucks over 20 years old, fitted at registration renewal or new licensing, reporting behaviour such as speeding to a monitoring centre. RTA planned to extend this to trucks ≥ 2.5 t including new vehicles, and to refuse registration or renewal without the device — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/news/uae/transport/monitoring-devices-must-for-all-heavy-trucks-from-august-1.2197763); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/transport/new-rules-for-heavy-trucks-in-dubai-starts-on-tuesday-1.2066289) (older articles; current scope unconfirmed)
- **Federal tracking:** owners of UAE-registered goods vehicles and trucks must register in a federal tracking system overseen by the **National Centre for Tracking Trucks and Shipments** under ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security) — summarised from [u.ae road safety](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/road-safety) (attribution approximate; legal instrument and device requirements not found)

### Inferences (what the software must do)
- **Vehicle master** with plate/emirate, Mulkiya (registration) number and expiry, chassis, axle configuration, GVW and tare, dimensions, insurance policy and expiry, inspection date, RTA telematics-device ID, ICP tracking registration, mobility/entry permits, and trailer pairing. Send **expiry reminders**, since Mulkiya renewal depends on valid insurance, a passed inspection and (in Dubai) a telematics device.
- **Load-weight check:** for each trip, compare declared cargo + tare with the axle-based gross limit for the truck/trailer combination, and warn before dispatch. Capture weighbridge tickets (expense + weight evidence) and reconcile them with the declared load.
- **Fine and incident register** for overload and dimension fines, linked to trips.
- The app's own **phone GPS tracking does not replace** RTA-mandated in-vehicle telematics or the ICP tracking system. It can complement them and may later integrate with them.

## 8. Data protection (PDPL – Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021; DIFC; ADGM)

### Takeaway
The federal PDPL has been in force since 2 Jan 2022, but its **Executive Regulations still appear unpublished in the Official Gazette as of mid/late 2026**. Vendor claims that they were issued with a 1 Jan 2027 compliance date are unverified. The PDPL is **consent-first** with enumerated exceptions, including contract performance and employment obligations, and has **no general "legitimate interests" basis**. Cross-border transfers need an adequate destination, a contract (SCC-type), or explicit consent. DIFC (DP Law No. 5 of 2020, amended July 2025) and ADGM (DP Regulations 2021) run separate GDPR-style regimes that apply to entities licensed there instead of the PDPL. Employee GPS tracking has no UAE-specific guidance, so the safe approach is notice plus documented necessity plus consent where feasible.

### Cited Findings
- PDPL entered into force on **2 Jan 2022**. The UAE Data Office is operational and issuing guidance. A de facto 72-hour breach-notification expectation has emerged. No private-sector supervisory authority has been clearly designated — [Recording Law](https://www.recordinglaw.com/world-laws/world-data-privacy-laws/uae-data-privacy-laws/); [DLA Piper Data Protection Laws of the World](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/countries/uae-general/law.html)
- **Executive Regulations status (conflict):** as of May 2026 they were not published as a discrete instrument in the Official Gazette — [Recording Law](https://www.recordinglaw.com/world-laws/world-data-privacy-laws/uae-data-privacy-laws/). Not on the legislation portal as of mid-2026 — [Aurosecurity](https://www.aurosecurity.com/gcc-data-privacy-laws-in-2026). Vendor blogs claim issuance in 2026 with full compliance by **1 Jan 2027** — [ITSecNow](https://itsecnow.com/regulators/pdpl-executive-regulations-2026); [PromptQuorum](https://www.promptquorum.com/local-llms/uae-pdpl-data-sovereignty-local-ai-2026) (unsourced to the Gazette)
- **Lawful bases:** processing without consent is prohibited except in enumerated cases (one source counts 10), including performance of a contract with the data subject and employment-law obligations. There is **no general legitimate-interests basis** — [Modulos](https://docs.modulos.ai/frameworks/uae-pdpl); [Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/en/blog/uae-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl-the-complete-business)
- The PDPL does not address employer-employee power imbalance in consent, and there is no explicit requirement that consent be "freely given" — [Chambers Data Protection 2025](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/view/158325293/509/); [Baker McKenzie Data & Cyber Handbook](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-data-and-cyber-handbook/emea/uae/topics/data-processing-in-the-employment-context)
- **Employee monitoring:** advisers recommend employee privacy notices, monitoring assessments, access controls and retention rules. If monitoring data is stored abroad, confirm the destination is adequate and tell employees. A right to object to certain monitoring is mentioned — [Al Kabban](https://alkabban.com/cyber-law/employee-monitoring-data/) (secondary)
- **Cross-border transfers (Arts. 22-23):** allowed to adequate jurisdictions. Otherwise allowed under a contract imposing PDPL-equivalent protections, or with the data subject's **explicit consent** where that does not conflict with UAE public or security interests. Implementing detail awaits the Executive Regulations — [Kayrouz & Associates](https://www.kayrouzandassociates.com/ar/insights/cross-border-data-transfers-under-uae-law-in-2026); [Simmons & Simmons FAQ](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/ckwugz8lu16rs0a44qvbqcbz9/uae-personal-data-protection-law-faqs)
- **DIFC / ADGM:** DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020 and ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021 are GDPR-aligned. ADGM provides SCCs, BCRs and an adequacy list following EU Commission decisions. DIFC amendments effective **15 July 2025** added documented adequacy assessments for transfers, a private right of action in DIFC Courts and higher fines — [Recording Law](https://www.recordinglaw.com/world-laws/world-data-privacy-laws/uae-data-privacy-laws/); [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/countries/uae-general/law.html)

### Inferences (what the software must do)
- **GPS tracking of staff phones:** the tenant (employer) is the controller and the SaaS is a processor. Build in: (1) a **driver privacy notice and explicit in-app consent capture** (timestamped, versioned, withdrawable); (2) a **work-hours-only / on-trip-only tracking mode**, with no off-duty tracking by default, to support the necessity argument under the employment and contract exceptions; (3) a visible tracking-on indicator on the phone; (4) role-based access to location history; (5) configurable **retention and auto-deletion** of raw GPS points (e.g., keep trip summaries for the 7-year financial record, purge raw pings sooner); (6) data-subject request tooling (access and export, correction, deletion where lawful).
- **Hosting and transfers:** offer **UAE-region hosting** (e.g., in-country cloud regions) as the default for UAE tenants. Sign a DPA with SCC-type clauses with each tenant. Keep a sub-processor list. Get explicit consent or adequacy justification for any transfer outside the UAE, including to an India-based dev and support team.
- **Breach response:** incident logging and a workflow that can notify tenants promptly, so they can meet the ~72-hour expectation.
- **DIFC/ADGM tenants** (rare for trucking, but possible for holding companies) need GDPR-style features: RoPA export, DPIA template, adequacy documentation.

### Gaps
- No primary PDPL text was read. Article numbers for exceptions (Art. 4) and transfers (Arts. 22-23) come from secondary sources.
- No UAE regulator guidance on employee GPS or phone tracking was found. The approach above is inference.
- Sector data-localisation rules (e.g., for telecoms or health) were not checked. Nothing found suggests general localisation for logistics data, but this is unconfirmed.
- One source mentioned a new "Federal Authority for AI and Data" (approved June 2026); single-sourced and unverified.
