## Free trial

The free trial costs nothing and needs no card. When it ends nothing is charged; your data stays, read-only, until you choose a plan.

## Monthly plans

You can stop a monthly plan at any time; it simply is not renewed and runs until the end of the month already paid. The month already started is not refunded.

## Yearly and lifetime plans

If you are not satisfied, ask for a refund within 7 days of the payment and we refund it in full. After 7 days a yearly plan runs to the end of its year and is not refunded.

## Mistakes

If you were charged twice or for the wrong plan, tell us and we will put it right at once.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back the way you paid, normally within 7 working days of our approval. Write to us from the contact page or at the support email with your company name and the payment reference.
